WHAT WE BUILD

Three disciplines, one bench. Everything is drawn, 3D-modeled, cast, and finished in-house — you approve the render before any metal is poured.

CHAINS

Cuban, Miami, rope, and Franco in 10k–18k gold. Each link soldered and filed by hand; clasps rated to the chain's true weight.

PENDANTS

Your logo, portrait, or idea carved into a one-off piece. Pavé, prong, or channel-set stones — layout plotted stone by stone.

GRILLZ

Molded from a dental impression we send to your door. Solid gold, open-face, or iced — polished so they sit flush and come off clean.

THE COMMISSION, STEP BY STEP

A real custom piece takes weeks, not days. Here's the honest timeline we run every build on.

  1. 1Consult & sketch

    You bring the idea — a drawing, a photo, a logo file. We talk size, metal, stones, and budget, then draw it up.

    Week 1
  2. 23D render approval

    You see the exact piece in a CAD render, down to stone counts and clasp. Nothing is cast until you sign off.

    Week 2
  3. 3Cast & build

    Lost-wax casting in your chosen karat, then assembly, soldering, and filing at the bench.

    Weeks 3–5
  4. 4Setting & finish

    Stones set by hand under a scope, then polish or ice-finish. Grillz get a final fit check against your mold.

    Week 6
  5. 5Weigh-in & handoff

    Your piece is weighed in front of you and delivered with its metal and stone documentation.

    Week 7
